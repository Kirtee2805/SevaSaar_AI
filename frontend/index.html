S SevaSaar AI (सेवासार)

An AI-powered multilingual patient intake assistant designed for Indian hospitals, OPD clinics, and rural Primary Healthcare Centers (PHCs). Supports Gujarati, Hindi, English, and Mixed language input.

⚠️ CLINICAL SAFETY DISCLAIMER

SevaSaar AI DOES NOT diagnose diseases, prescribe medicines, or provide medical treatment plans. It strictly structures patient complaints and flags emergency safety indicators for human medical triage.

🛡️ Built with Gemma & LangChain Architecture

Lightweight Python FastAPI backend with LangChain orchestration and Gemma AI multilingual inference. Frontend engineered in pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.
